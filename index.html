How to use this tool

This tool lets anyone on the Nexus Concepts team build a professional client quotation in minutes — from a phone, tablet or computer. It also shows your margin privately, which never appears on the client PDF.

1

Tap "Build quote" at the top

This is where you fill in all the job details. Everything updates live as you type.

2

Enter the client name and property address

Type these in the first section. They appear on the quote automatically. Add a reference number if you want — for example: NCC / 007 / 2026.

3

Enter the property measurements

This is the most important step. Enter the floor area, ceiling areas, number of bathrooms, doors, and so on — all in square metres. The tool pre-fills sensible defaults based on property type, but real measurements give a more accurate quote. You can get these from the property listing or floor plan.

4

Tick what work needs to be done

Scroll down and tick every item that applies. Work is grouped by area — Floors, Ceilings, Doors, Kitchen, Bathrooms, Paint, Outdoor, Electrical. Some boxes tick automatically when they always go together.

5

Check the total in the bar at the bottom

As you tick items, the grand total updates in the bar at the bottom of the screen. Tap "Preview" at the top to see the full client quote, or tap "Margin" to see your profit breakdown — that section is for internal use only and never prints.

6

Save as PDF and send to the client

Go to the Preview tab and tap "Save as PDF". In the print dialog, set destination to "Save as PDF". The margin dashboard is automatically hidden from the printed file — the client only sees the professional quotation.

This is an estimate, not a final price

Numbers are based on your actual contractor rates and scale with the measurements you enter. Always tell the client: "This is our indicative estimate — we confirm the exact figure after a site survey."

About the margin figures

Cost figures come from your actual vendor quotes — Yashfa Technical Services (tiling, ceilings, electrical, sanitary, paint), Taj Al Faris Carpentry (doors and wardrobes), and Envisage (kitchen). Update the tool whenever your contractor rates change.
